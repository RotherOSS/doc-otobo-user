Search
======

OTOBO offers several ways to find information.

- The :doc:`Ticket Search <tickets/search>` finds tickets based on various criteria like state, queue, owner or dynamic fields.
- The :doc:`FAQ Search <faq/search>` finds FAQ articles based on various criteria like category, language or keywords.
- The :doc:`search/elastic` finds tickets, customers, customer users and config items while you type.
  It is only available if Elasticsearch is activated in your system.
